## Prep gates (before the timed run, done across prior runs)

1. Reach the Boss's Office **~3–4 times**, entering **2845** before the Narrator finishes speaking, so the Narrator eventually **auto-opens the fireplace** on entry (eliminates the ~15-sec keypad-music wait). `spoiler: progression`
2. Reset until you **spawn in a short-spawn corridor** (blue-office or 3-door hall layout) — reduces the initial walking segment. Reset up to ~15 times. `spoiler: progression`

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` SEQ-002: the fireplace auto-open is a cross-run prep condition also documented in the Freedom Ending gate sequence.

## The timed run (P2-confirmed splits)

3. **LEFT door** → **stairwell UP** → **Boss's Office** (fireplace already open; just walk through). `spoiler: progression`
4. **Elevator DOWN** → in the Mind Control Facility, climb the **chair → desk** in the Monitor Room and **jump from the railing to the first bridge** (Monitor Room skip — skips the first-door wait entirely). `spoiler: progression`
5. Press the facility buttons → **elevator UP** → **Power Room** → press **OFF** → step outside before 4:22. `spoiler: story`

**Timing context (P2):** The achievement threshold is 4:22 (excludes load times). Confirmed mid-pack Freedom Glitchless leaderboard times sit around 4:29 (e.g. ToranPetto 4m 29s ranked 7th), so the actual WR is faster than 4:22 — the threshold is achievable with the prep steps above even without optimal execution. Consult https://www.speedrun.com/tspud (Freedom IL, Glitchless) for current verified leader.

## If it fails
Confirm the fireplace auto-opens (needs ≥3 prior Boss's Office visits entering 2845 early). Confirm you spawned in the short corridor (not the full-length standard layout). Ensure you are NOT holding the Bucket. Use the Monitor Room chair/desk skip to shave additional seconds.

## Achievement binding
- **Speed run** (`ach: speed_run`) → this route under 4:22. **Beat the game** uses the identical Freedom Ending route without the time constraint. Do NOT hold the Bucket (bucket disqualifies both achievements). See `achievements.md`.
