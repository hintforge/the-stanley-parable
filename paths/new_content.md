## Unlock gate

Door 416 in the corridor becomes the **New Content** door once you have earned enough ending-points:
- **6 points** if you answered "yes, played before" at the start (door can appear as early as the 3rd run).
- **15 points** if you answered "no" (as early as the 4th run).

Content-warning skips still count toward this threshold.

## The chain (each step unlocks the next)

1. **New Content door** → ride → **Jump Circle** (36 jumps) → "thanks for enjoying the new content" sign → reset. (**New Content Ending**)
2. Vent into the **Memory Zone** → Steam reviews → press the **Skip Button** ~15-16 times until it appears broken on the ground → desert wasteland. (**Skip Button / Memory Zone Ending**) The Memory Zone also contains the Serious Room Easter egg.
3. **"NEW New Content"** neon door → **TSP2 Expo Hall** → Narrator pitches a sequel; permanently adds bucket/figurines/jumps. (**Sequel Ending**)
4. In the Expo, jump into the **Infinite Hole 4×**. (**Infinite Hole Ending** -- does not return to the office.)
5. Collect all **6 figurines** (see `items/collectibles.md`), then reset → Narrator recollects them forward then in reverse. (**Figurines / Collectibles Ending**)
6. After the Figurines Ending + reboot/Settings Person ~5×, **"Epilogue"** appears on the main menu. (**Epilogue**)
7. Return to the Expo and flip the **"Free Achievement" lever** → **Test achievement please ignore** (only works post-Epilogue).

## Notes
- The **Cheese Trigger** checkbox changes the New Content ride narrator to mod "GrilledCheese."
- After the Epilogue the game lets you name a new sequel (TSP2, TSP3…) each boot.

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-003: the Cheese Trigger checkbox is activated backstage during the Press Conference Ending and persists into the New Content ride.
