## Spine route (to the Freedom Ending)

Two Doors → **left** → Meeting/Conference Room → stairwell **up** to the **Boss's Office** → keypad **2845** → fireplace opens → elevator **down** → **Mind Control Facility** → press **OFF** → step outside. This is the canonical "Freedom" route and the basis for **Beat the game** and **Speed run**.

## Branch points off the spine

- **Boss's stairway -- go DOWN instead of up** → looping paradox rooms → **Mariella / Dream Ending**.
- **Boss's Office -- back out before the doors close** (traps the Narrator) → Door 428 opens → room 754 → escape pod floor 760 → **Escape Pod Ending**. Default back-out window is 0.5s; Low Dexterity Mode widens it to 3.5s.
- **Boss's Office keypad -- enter 8 eight times (8888 ×2)** instead of 2845 → **8888888888888888** achievement.
- **Mind Control Facility -- press ON** → **Countdown Ending**.
- **Mind Control Facility -- take the ESCAPE hall** → crush machine → female-Narrator rescue → **Museum Ending**.
- **Monitor Room -- climb chair→desk→over the railing, fall** → **Bottom of the Mind Control Room Ending**.
- **Boss's secret elevator -- ride up/down 3×** → **Press Conference Ending**.
- **After the Meeting Room -- enter the Broom Closet and wait** → **Broom Closet Ending**.
- **Heaven Ending** -- click 5 "Awaiting Input" computers in order across separate runs (419 → 423 → Secretary's → 434 → Stanley's), resetting between each.
