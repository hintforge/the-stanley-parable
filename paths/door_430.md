## The chain (P2-confirmed click counts)

Per-door click counts corroborated across two independent sources (PSNProfiles and xboxachievements/ScreenRant):

1. **Door 430** — click **5** times. `spoiler: none`
2. **Door 430** — click **20** more times. `spoiler: progression`
3. **Door 430** — click **50** more times (75 total on Door 430 so far). `spoiler: progression`
4. **Door 417** — click **~20** times (down the left corridor, next open door on the left). `spoiler: progression`
5. **Door 437** — click **a few** times (next to 430). `spoiler: progression`
6. **Door 415** — click **~10** times (hallway past 417). `spoiler: progression`
7. Return to **Door 437** — no additional click needed. `spoiler: progression`
8. **Copy machine** (behind the 431–436 partition) — click **once**. `spoiler: progression`
9. Climb **Employee 419's desk** via the box → chair → desk (no jump mechanic). `spoiler: progression`
10. **Door 416** — click **a few** times. If Door 416 has been replaced by the New Content door, go to the Boss's Office and back to restore the original Door 416 mid-chain. `spoiler: progression`
11. Return to the **copy machine** — click **once** more. `spoiler: progression`
12. **Door 430** — click **5** final times → achievement pops. `spoiler: progression`

## Critical caveats

- **Do NOT enter the Two Doors Room mid-chain** — this resets the Narrator's chain state.
- **New Content door bug (still present as of P2 research):** If the New Content door has replaced Door 416, going to the Boss's Office and back can restore the original Door 416 mid-chain. The safest approach is to complete the chain on a fresh run before accumulating enough ending-points for the New Content door to appear (~6 or ~15 points depending on your "played before" answer).

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-006: the New Content door replacing Door 416 blocks step 10 of this chain; workaround documented above.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` SEQ-004: entering the Two Doors Room mid-chain resets all chain progress.
