## Branch points

- **Right → Maintenance Room → down the small elevator** → **Confusion Ending**. (The Maintenance Room is also the re-rail: from here a right-door player can reach virtually all left-door endings.)
- **Right → Warehouse:**
  - Drop to the bottom of the warehouse → **Powerful Ending**.
  - Cargo lift -- step on, back off, then jump down → **Cold Feet Ending**.
  - Crouch around boxes → fall to a shelf → into the **Vent** → recorder → **Tape Recorder Ending**.
  - Ride the lift to the end → **Phone Room** → answer the phone → **Apartment Ending**.
- **Phone Room -- unplug the phone** → "Choice" video → return to the Two Doors; alternate both doors → **Not Stanley / Incorrect Ending** (player leaves Stanley's body, credits roll).
- **Colored Doors Room:**
  - **Red door** → looping hall → Star Observatory → fall off the stairs 4× → **Zending**.
  - **Blue door ×3** → fail the Baby Game → minigames → **Games / Playtest Ending**.
  - Play the Baby Game for the full **four hours** → **Art Ending** (after two hours a puppy/piranhas object is added).
  - Enter while **holding the Bucket** → "Is this... A Bucket?" quiz → **No Buckets Ending**.

## Notes
- Content-warning skips (Zending) still count as completed endings toward the New Content threshold.
- The Bucket is required for No Buckets and is only available after the Sequel Ending (`endings/bucket_variants.md`).
