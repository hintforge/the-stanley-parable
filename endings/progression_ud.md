# Endings -- Ultra Deluxe Progression Chain

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02
**research_run:** P1 + P2 (2026-06-02)

The Ultra Deluxe-exclusive chain. Each ending unlocks the next; the chain is gated by cross-playthrough state (see `mechanics.md` and `paths/new_content.md`). This is the chain the **Test achievement please ignore** achievement requires you to complete in full.

| New Content Ending | Earn **6** ending-points (answered "yes, played before") or **15** points (answered "no") → Door 416 becomes "New Content" → ride → Jump Circle (36 jumps) → "thanks for enjoying the new content" sign → reset | late-game | No |

## Notes

- **New Content threshold (6 vs 15 points)** is tied to your answer to "have you played before?" -- only the Fandom wiki and Russian/Steam guides spell this out. In run-count terms, the New Content door can appear as early as the **3rd run** (answered yes) or the **4th run** (answered no). [What mainstream English guides miss]
  _source: P1 deep-research 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_
- **Content-warning skips still count as completed endings** toward the New Content threshold (applies to the Dream Ending and the Zending).
- The **Paywall Ending** does not exist on the Steam product; it is iOS-free-trial-only. Excluded from the 45 accessible PC endings.
- After the **Epilogue**, the Test-achievement "Free Achievement" lever in the Expo becomes functional, and the game lets you name a new sequel (TSP2, TSP3…) each boot.

## Achievement binding
- **Test achievement please ignore** -- flip the "Free Achievement" lever in the TSP2 Expo, which only works after completing the Epilogue. The full prerequisite chain runs through this entire file. See `achievements.md`.

### New Content Ending gate sequence
**Branch root:** Door 416 replaced by the "New Content" sign in the corridor.
**Access note:** Unlocks at **6 ending-points** (answered "yes, played before" to the Settings Person) or **15 points** (answered "no"); appears on the ~3rd run (yes) or ~4th run (no).
**Estimated run time:** ~8 min.

1. Accumulate required ending-points across prior runs (see locks-and-keys for per-answer threshold). `spoiler: progression`
2. On a fresh run, head toward the Two Doors Room; the **New Content** door (former Door 416) is ajar in the corridor. `spoiler: progression`
3. Enter and ride the maintenance cart through the Ultra Deluxe presentation. `spoiler: story`
4. Take the slow elevator UP to the **Jump Circle** (press Space 36 times inside the tape circle on the floor). `spoiler: story`
5. Follow the back hallway → second elevator → "Thank you for enjoying the New Content" sign. `spoiler: story`
6. Narrator rages and resets to the Memory Zone → auto-loops. `spoiler: story`

**Branch notes:** Cannot be reached while holding the bucket on the first unlock. After the Epilogue, replayable from the Executive Bathroom. Cart narrator voice changes if the Cheese Trigger is active.

### Skip Button Ending gate sequence
**Branch root:** Memory Zone vent, immediately after the New Content Ending resets.
**Access note:** Triggered directly after the New Content Ending.
**Estimated run time:** ~8 min.

1. After the New Content reset, follow the Narrator and **crawl through the vent** on the left; take the stairs back to the office. `spoiler: progression`
2. Follow the Memory Zone path until you reach the room with a Skip Button review mentioning a skip feature. `spoiler: story`
3. Press the **Skip Button** ~15–16 times until it breaks. `spoiler: story`
4. Time fast-forwards; the room decays into a desert wasteland → auto-loops. `spoiler: story`

**Branch notes:** This desert is where the Epilogue later resumes. Triggers the New New Content neon door for the Sequel Ending.

### Sequel Ending gate sequence
**Branch root:** "New New Content" neon door (former Door 416 location), after the Skip Button Ending.
**Access note:** After completing the Skip Button Ending.
**Estimated run time:** ~10 min.

1. On a new run, the neon **New New Content** door is open where Door 416 was. `spoiler: progression`
2. Walk the TSP2 Expo of proposed sequel mechanics; pick up the first **figurine** (auto-given at the collectibles stand). `spoiler: story`
3. Optionally pull the "free achievement" lever (non-functional until after the Epilogue). `spoiler: story`
4. The Narrator decides to fold the sequel ideas into the original game; resets with new mechanics permanently added → auto-loops. `spoiler: story`

**Branch notes:** This is the gate that unlocks the **Reassurance Bucket** (appears at desk ~422 in the second office room on all future runs) AND permanent jumps and figurines.

### Infinite Hole Ending gate sequence
**Branch root:** TSP2 Expo — bottomless pit exhibit.
**Access note:** During the Sequel/Expo visit (after the Skip Button Ending).
**Estimated run time:** ~5 min.

1. In the Expo, go past the giant Stanley statue; follow signs to the **Infinity Hole**. `spoiler: progression`
2. Jump into the hole **~4 times** (it shrinks each time until Stanley is trapped). `spoiler: story`
3. The Narrator abandons Stanley; press buttons to change visuals; the Narrator returns and resets to the Expo entrance → auto-loops to the Expo entrance (the ONLY ending that does NOT return to Room 427). `spoiler: story`

**Branch notes:** The bucket can be brought but changes nothing visible (appears beside Stanley in the hole).

### Figurines Ending gate sequence
**Branch root:** Collect all 6 Stanley figurines, then restart.
**Access note:** Post-Sequel; figurines appear around the office after the Sequel Ending.
**Estimated run time:** Multi-run collection; order flexible.

Figurine locations (collect across runs in any order):
1. **Figurine #1** — TSP2 Expo collectibles stand (auto on first Expo visit). `spoiler: progression`
2. **Figurine #2** — Warehouse, left of the cargo lift, across the wooden planks. `spoiler: progression`
3. **Figurine #3** — bottom of the stairs leading up to the Boss's Office (go down). `spoiler: progression`
4. **Figurine #4** — Boss's Office secret passage, dark alcove opposite the elevator. `spoiler: progression`
5. **Figurine #5** — Executive Bathroom (turn left at the top of the stairs). `spoiler: progression`
6. **Figurine #6** — Warehouse blue/red doors room, open room on the right wall. `spoiler: progression`
7. After collecting 6/6, restart → the Narrator obsesses, relives each collection (inflating the count above 6/6), then retires Stanley → auto-loops. `spoiler: story`

**Branch notes:** Triggers the "REBOOT THE GAME ENTIRELY" whiteboard in the Meeting Room — the cue to start the Settings-Person reboot sequence for the Epilogue.

### Epilogue gate sequence
**Branch root:** Main-menu "Epilogue" option (appears after prerequisites are complete).
**Access note:** Requires New Content + Skip Button + Sequel + Figurines endings completed, the Reassurance Bucket obtained, AND the full game rebooted enough times to exhaust the Settings Person's surveys.
**Estimated run time:** ~15 min.

Prerequisite chain (done across multiple sessions):
1. Complete the New Content, Skip Button, Sequel, and Figurines endings and obtain the Reassurance Bucket. `spoiler: progression`
2. Fully quit the game and relaunch; the Settings Person appears at startup with a survey question. Repeat relaunches until he says he has "learned enough" and bids farewell. ~5 relaunches is the reliable upper bound (some users report 4 launches after the survey text appears 3×; see Caveats). `spoiler: progression`
3. On the next relaunch, **Epilogue** appears on the main menu — select it. `spoiler: story`

Epilogue run:
4. Walk the desert through several phases to the ruined Memory Zone; pass the levitating figurines into the Underground Office. `spoiler: story`
5. Interact with the Settings Person's computer; **agree** to the number-increase plan → unlocks *Test achievement please ignore* and repairs the Expo trophy machine. `spoiler: story`
6. Ending resolves → auto-loops to Room 427. `spoiler: story`

**Branch notes:** "REBOOT THE GAME ENTIRELY" whiteboard (post-Figurines Ending) is the in-game cue. After completing the Epilogue, the Free Achievement lever in the Expo is functional. The bucket can be brought to the Epilogue only via the Bucket Escape Pod in the same session.
