# Endings -- Bucket Variants

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02
**research_run:** P1 + P2 (2026-06-02)

After the **Sequel Ending** unlocks The Stanley Parable 2, the **Reassurance Bucket** permanently appears in the second office room. Carrying it produces a distinct "with bucket" variant of nearly every original and UD ending.

## What unlocks bucket variants

- The Bucket is unlocked after the **Sequel Ending** and appears at the start of subsequent runs in the second office area.
- Bring it almost anywhere for a bucket-variant ending. Count: **~15 bucket variants** (14 old + 1 new), per the Russian wiki / showgamer count. The exact tally varies by source (medium confidence -- bucket-variant counting differs between guides).
- The **No Buckets Ending** specifically requires carrying the Bucket into the Colored Doors Room (see `endings/right_door.md`).
- The **Bucket Escape Pod Ending** removes the bucket from its podium across the two following restarts (a Replacement Bucket appears).

See `items/collectibles.md` for the Reassurance Bucket object details.

## Bucket gate-list pattern (P2)

Every bucket variant follows the same structural pattern as the base ending's gate sequence, with one additional gate inserted before Gate 1:

**Gate 0 (add to any bucket variant run):** Pick up the Reassurance Bucket from the second office room (post-Sequel, near desk ~422) and carry it throughout the entire run. `spoiler: progression · puzzle-tier: 0`

After inserting Gate 0, follow the base ending's gate sequence exactly. The bucket produces a distinct variant conclusion instead of the base ending's conclusion.

**Endings with meaningful bucket variants:**
Freedom (trapped in Power Room), Countdown → Silly Birds, Bottom of Mind Control Room (live in the pit), Museum (Bucket Museum), Dream/Mariella (everyday-bucket madness), Escape Pod (working pod launches the bucket — also depletes the podium bucket for 2 restarts), Confusion → Bucket Destroyer, Powerful (die together), Cold Feet (cushioning reference), Tape Recorder (200+-day Reassurance Bucket recording), Apartment (Bucket apartment), Incorrect/Not Stanley (comedic timing), Out of Map → Gambhorra'ta, New Content (Narrator notices you saw the sequel), No Buckets (colored-door bucket substitute).

**Endings with no meaningful bucket variant:** Whiteboard, Heaven, Infinite Hole (bucket appears beside Stanley but changes nothing), Broom Closet.

**Colored-door endings are blocked by the bucket:** Attempting to enter the Colored Doors Room while holding the bucket routes to the No Buckets Ending instead of Zending / Games / Art.

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-005: carrying the Reassurance Bucket into the Colored Doors Room blocks all three colored-door endings and forces the No Buckets Ending instead.
