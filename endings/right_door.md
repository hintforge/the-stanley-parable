## Notes

- The **Maintenance Room** re-rails a right-door player back onto left-door content -- it is the bridge that lets a disobedient player reach virtually all Narrator-compliant endings. See `mechanics.md` (Narrator mechanic).
- **Not Stanley / Incorrect Ending** carries a major reveal: the Narrator discovers that the *player* (not Stanley) holds control. _Thematic payoff._
  _source: P1 deep-research 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: lore · spoiler: story_
- **Apartment Ending's** narrative meaning is a story-defining beat; the route itself (answer the phone) is progression-tier.
- The **Art Ending** is an endurance gag (four real hours of the Baby Game); after two hours a second object (puppy/piranhas) is added to keep alive.
- The **No Buckets Ending** requires carrying the Reassurance Bucket (UD-only); see `endings/bucket_variants.md` and `items/collectibles.md`.
- **Games / Playtest** content differs between HD (Portal) and UD (Rocket League).

### Confusion Ending gate sequence
**Branch root:** Two Doors Room → RIGHT door → Maintenance Room → small elevator down.
**Access note:** Any run; involves scripted restarts.
**Estimated run time:** ~12 min.

1. Leave Room 427; walk to the Two Doors Room. `spoiler: none`
2. Take the **RIGHT** door into the Employee Lounge. `spoiler: none`
3. Walk through the lounge. `spoiler: none`
4. Take the first door on the **LEFT** in the corridor into the Maintenance Room. `spoiler: progression`
5. Ride the small Maintenance elevator **DOWN**. `spoiler: progression`
6. The Narrator gets lost; scripted restarts fire; the Two Doors Room gains six doors; the Adventure Line™ appears. `spoiler: story`
7. Follow / leave the Adventure Line™ to the Confusion Ending Schedule room; final reset. `spoiler: story`

**Branch notes:** Adventure Line™ persists into the next restart (a tracked ending). With the bucket → Bucket Destroyer variant. Auto-loops through the scripted resets.

### Incorrect / Not Stanley Ending gate sequence
**Branch root:** Phone Room — unplug the phone; then explore both doors.
**Access note:** Any run.
**Estimated run time:** ~8 min.

1–4. Apartment gates 1–4 (reach the Phone Room).
5. **Unplug** the phone at the outlet on the back wall. `spoiler: progression`
6. Watch the Choice video; return to the Two Doors Room. `spoiler: progression`
7. Go through **BOTH** doors at least once (right: narrative contradiction; left: voice-box password Stanley can't speak). `spoiler: story`
8. You (the Player) leave Stanley's body above the ceiling; credits roll → auto-loops. `spoiler: story`

**Branch notes:** The order of doors changes Narrator dialogue. One of the most meta endings in the game.

### Zending gate sequence
**Branch root:** Colored Doors Room — Red door.
**Access note:** Any run; **requires NO bucket** (bucket blocks entry to the Colored Doors Room).
**Estimated run time:** ~7 min.

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-005: carrying the Reassurance Bucket here routes to the No Buckets Ending instead of Zending, Games, or Art.

4. Board the cargo lift; as it rises and moves forward, step off **RIGHT** onto the catwalk below. `spoiler: progression`
5. Reach the Colored Doors Room; enter the **RED** door. `spoiler: progression`
6. Looping hallway → Star Observatory; relax. `spoiler: story`
7. Leave the dome; at the staircase, fall **4 times** → auto-loops (or rest for a "Zen" outcome). `spoiler: story`

**Branch notes:** Entering with the bucket → No Buckets Ending instead. Content-warning skippable.

### No Buckets Ending gate sequence
**Branch root:** Enter the Colored Doors Room while carrying the Reassurance Bucket.
**Access note:** Post-Sequel only (bucket required).
**Estimated run time:** ~6 min.

1. Carry the Reassurance Bucket; RIGHT door → Warehouse → catwalk toward the Colored Doors Room. `spoiler: progression`
2. Attempting to enter with the bucket triggers the Narrator to divert Stanley to the "Is this... A Bucket?" quiz show. `spoiler: story`
3. Answer quiz prompts (outcome unaffected); the Narrator deletes all buckets, revealing everything was a bucket → auto-loops. `spoiler: story`

**Branch notes:** This is the bucket-substitute for all three colored-door endings (Zending / Games / Art), which the bucket otherwise blocks.
