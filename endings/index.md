## How to use this index

1. Ask the companion about a specific ending by name or description.
2. Ask "what endings have I unlocked?" to track progress.
3. Ask "what's the ending where [description]?" for discovery help.
4. Ask "how do I get to [ending name]?" -- routes to `paths/` for the decision tree.

## Missability note

**No ending is permanently missable.** Every ending is replayable by resetting and making different choices. Some UD endings unlock progressively across playthroughs (the New Content chain), and the 6 figurines require multiple resets, but nothing closes permanently. See `sections/missables.md`.
