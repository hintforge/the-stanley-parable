# Items -- Index

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02

The Stanley Parable has no traditional items or inventory system. This folder documents the Ultra Deluxe collectibles only. All standard item categories (weapons, consumables, crafting materials, abilities, upgrades, support items, builds) are absent.

## Files in this folder

- `collectibles.md` -- the 6 Stanley figurines ("Figleys") and the Reassurance Bucket (both UD-exclusive, unlocked after the Sequel Ending).

## Always-empty categories

The following categories from the standard hintforge framework are absent in The Stanley Parable and will never be populated:
- weapons
- consumables
- crafting materials
- abilities
- upgrades
- support items
- builds / loadouts
