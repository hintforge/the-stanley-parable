# Items -- Collectibles (Ultra Deluxe)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02
**research_run:** P1 (2026-06-02)

The only collectibles in the game: the **6 Stanley figurines ("Figleys")** and the **Reassurance Bucket**. Both are Ultra Deluxe-exclusive and unlock only after the **Sequel Ending** (TSP2).

## The 6 Stanley figurines

Unlocked only after completing the Sequel Ending. The first is auto-collected; the other 5 spawn around the office and require resets (they **cannot all be gathered in one run**). Resetting does not lose progress. The Meeting-room whiteboard gives hints, crossed off as collected.

| # | Specific path + room + action | Access window |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | TSP2 Expo Hall → Collectibles exhibit ("Collect Them All", giant rotating Stanley) → walk into the light | Auto-collected during the Expo Hall run |
| 2 | Right door → Employee Lounge → Warehouse; climb the plank ramp left of the cargo lift, drop onto the plank bridge → figurine at the end | Any run after TSP2 unlock |
| 3 | Left door → Meeting Room → stairwell to Boss's Office; go DOWN to the bottom of the stairs → under the stairs | Any run |
| 4 | Boss's Office → enter 2845 → fireplace opens → turn around (away from lift) → darkened alcove right of the elevator. **Reset after this one.** | Reset required after |
| 5 | Right door → Warehouse → ride the cargo lift, drop onto the catwalk → Red/Blue Doors room → open room to the immediate right (on a desk chair). **Cannot be collected while holding the Bucket.** | Reset required |
| 6 | Up the Boss's stairs, turn LEFT (not right to the office) → **Executive Washroom**. Best collected BEFORE #5, since #5's path can lock it out. | Reset required |

After all 6 → **Figurines Ending** → (reboot ~5× + Settings Person) → **Epilogue** unlocks. Recollection during the Figurines Ending can push the count to 18/6 (or up to 20 with a timed reset).

> Routing tip: collect **#6 before #5**, since #5's path can lock out #6.

## Reassurance Bucket

Unlocked after the Sequel Ending; appears in the second office area at the start of runs. Bring it almost anywhere for a **bucket-variant ending** (see `endings/bucket_variants.md`). The Bucket Escape Pod Ending removes the bucket from its podium across the two following restarts (a Replacement Bucket appears).
