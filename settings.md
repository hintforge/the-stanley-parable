# The Stanley Parable -- Settings

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02
**research_run:** P1 (2026-06-02)

Complete settings menu, organized by the game's in-game tabs. The **Settings World Champion** achievement requires setting **every numeric slider to every value** and cycling **every option**. Holding a key skips numbers in steps of 10, so click individually. **Hidden sliders appear only when their checkbox is enabled** -- those are the most-missed entries.

## General → Gameplay
- Field of View (50-90)
- Camera Sensitivity (0-10)
- Invert Y (toggle)
- Controller Vibration (toggle)

## General → Presentation
- Language (12 subtitle languages: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, Korean, Japanese)
- Show Subtitles (toggle)
- Subtitle Size (Very Small-Very Large)
- **Subtitle Background Opacity (0-100)** -- *hidden until Show Subtitles is on*

## General → Accessibility
- Show Translation Labels (toggle)
- **Translation Label Background Opacity (0-100)** -- *hidden until enabled*
- Color Labels / colorblind toggle (Colored Doors Room)
- Content Warnings (toggle; **F** to skip on PC)
- Low Dexterity Mode (toggle) -- widens the Escape Pod back-out window from 0.5s to 3.5s
  > **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-001: enabling this setting significantly increases the Escape Pod Ending's back-out timing window.

## Settings World Champion checklist
Set every slider above to every value and cycle every option, **including the hidden Subtitle/Translation opacity sliders** (enable their checkboxes first). Some players report needing all toggles + video options too.

**Reward:** earning the achievement unlocks the **Settings World Champion room** in the TSP2 Expo and the **bumpscosity** setting -- the only unlockable setting in the franchise; "cannot be explained, adjustable on a slider in The Stanley Parable 2."
