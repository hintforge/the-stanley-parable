Cross-cutting summary files for The Stanley Parable. The game is narrative-no-nav with no spatial chapters; "sections" here are the cross-cutting catalogs (missables, story notes) rather than per-zone walkthroughs. Per-path detail lives in `paths/`; per-ending detail in `endings/`.

## Files in this folder

- `missables.md` -- missables summary. **Resolved: this game has no missable content.**
- `story_notes.md` -- cross-cutting thematic reveals (story-tier; gated behind the puzzle dial).

## How to use

- Ask "what am I about to miss?" -- consults `missables.md` (answer: nothing is permanently missable).
- Ask "what is this game actually about?" / "what happened in [ending]?" -- consults `story_notes.md` (behind spoiler tier) and the relevant `endings/` file.
- Ask "how do I get to [ending]?" -- routes to `paths/`.
