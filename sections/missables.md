**No missable content in this game -- all collectibles are always-available or absent.**

Specifically:
- **No achievement is missable.** Xbox/PSN roadmaps explicitly list "Missable achievements: No." The only gates are real-world time (Commitment, Super Go Outside) and a long prerequisite chain (Test achievement please ignore) -- none are loss windows. See `achievements.md`.
- **No ending is permanently missable.** Every ending is replayable by resetting and choosing differently. See `endings/index.md`.
- **The 6 figurines are not missable.** They require multiple resets (cannot all be gathered in one run) but resetting never loses progress, and they remain available across runs once unlocked. See `items/collectibles.md`.
